What .pulse does

Moderation

Warnings, bans, timeouts and kicks with numbered cases, reasons, moderators and a full audit trail.

Tickets

Support ticket channels with types, priorities, assignment, history, notes and close/reopen workflow.

Server management

Prefixes, logging channels, staff roles, module toggles — persisted per server, effective immediately.

Dashboard

Live overview, search and filters, case details, settings forms and safe confirmations for destructive actions.

How it works

  1. Access the dashboardHead to the login page when you're ready.
  2. Sign in with DiscordOAuth with the identify and guilds scopes only.
  3. Pick a serverOnly servers you can manage — and where .pulse is installed — can be opened.
  4. Manage everythingTickets, moderation, logs and settings, all live.
